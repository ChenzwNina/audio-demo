Emotion Demo

Compare speech generated with and without specific emotions

Emotions are selected from Plutchik's Wheel of Emotions

Note: I tested the eight emotions from Plutchik’s Wheel of Emotions using ElevenLabs emotion tags, mostly using emotion terms from the middle ring. Overall, the tags worked well for most emotions. Adding “!” can also strengthen the emotional expression (e.g., see terror and admiration). However, some nuances were difficult to distinguish. For example, disgust and boredom were hard to differentiate, and admiration and trust were not very obvious unless strengthened with “!”.
