Cut-offs

Exploring interruptions and cut-offs in dialogue

Dialogue Demo

Person 1: Um... I think climate change is, like, becoming harder to ignore these days.
Person 1: [Concern] We're kind of seeing more extreme weather events, you know—
Person 2: [interrupting!] —like floods... Sorry go ahead—
Person 1: —and we should take actions!
Note: I used the text-to-dialogue feature in ElevenLabs in the current demo, which generates all transcript first and inputs as a block, so they are not taking turns. What we can also explore is taking turns, and deciding rules to manually control cut-off since now latency is almost 0.
